The members’ app

App support

What the app is

The Rustnuts app is the members’ app of Rustnuts Social Motorcycle Club Incorporated (Victoria, Australia, incorporation A0119901K). Members use it for the ride calendar, club chats, meetings and votes, fees and the club’s records. It is built and run by the club for its members, FNGs, supporters and their families.

How accounts work

  • You sign in with your email address. The app emails you a one-time code; there are no passwords.
  • New to the club? Tap Ask to join and fill in the form. Your chapter’s board reviews it and approves members; until then you see the waiting screen.
  • Members’ families get a family view: the home screen, the ride calendar and their own details, but not the chats or other members’ details.

Anyone who opens the app without signing in sees only the club’s public information: chapters, sponsors and club links.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Me (applicants: the waiting screen), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. Without the app, email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au from the address you sign in with. Deleting the app account isn’t resigning from the club.

What deleting your account does

Contact

Problems signing in, something not working, a question about your details: email apps@rustnutssmc.com.au. We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Membership questions (joining, fees, standing down) go to your chapter Secretary.

Your information

What the app collects, why, who sees it and where it goes is set out in the privacy policy.
