Group riding procedures, illustrated

How we ride together and stay safe. These diagrams show the club’s group riding procedures: who does what in the pack, formation, hand signals, passing, turns, lane changes and stops.

Members read the full written Road Rules and sign them each year. Road law applies at all times, and nothing here gives anyone right of way. Tap a diagram to see it full size.
